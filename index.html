Phát âm · pronunciation

Sounds & basics

The 29 letters

Each letter has a name (used when spelling out loud: "bê, a, huyền: bà") and a sound taught in Vietnamese schools ("bờ"). Tap either to hear it.

Vietnamese has no f, j, w or z; they appear only in foreign words. The tone marks sit on top of these letters: see Tones.

Consonants at the start of a word

Where Saigon and Hanoi differ, the Hanoi sound is shown so you can recognise both. You are learning the Saigon one.

Sounds at the end of a word

Final consonants are never released: the mouth closes, but no puff of air comes out. Saigon also merges several endings.

The 12 vowels

ă and â are short versions of a and ơ. ư has no English match: say "oo" while smiling.

Vowel pairs

ia/iê, ua/uô and ưa/ươ are the same sound; the spelling changes when a consonant follows (bia, tiền).

Read any date

Vietnamese writes dates day / month / year.

Counting

See Usage → Catch the number to practise prices, times and phone numbers.

Days of the week

Weekdays are numbered: thứ hai ("second") is Monday. Signs abbreviate them T2 to T7, and CN for Sunday.

Order

Months

April is tháng tư, never tháng bốn. In the lunar calendar, month 1 is tháng giêng and month 12 is tháng chạp.

Years

Say "năm" before a year: năm hai ngàn không trăm hai mươi sáu. Older years are often shortened: năm chín mươi = 1990.

Calendar words

Lunar calendar

The first ten days of a lunar month use mùng: mùng một Tết is the first day of Lunar New Year.

Zodiac years · 12 con giáp

The same 12 kanji as the Japanese 干支, read in Sino-Vietnamese. Vietnam has the cat where Japan has the rabbit. 2026 is năm Ngọ, the year of the horse.

Thanh điệu · the tones

Five tones in the South, not six

Saigon speech merges hỏi and ngã into one dipping-rising tone. Pitch is drawn on a 1–5 scale, low to high. Tap a word to hear it.

How Southern numbers work

21, 31, 41… one becomes mốt: hai mươi mốt.

24, 34… four is usually tư: hai mươi tư.

15, 25… five becomes lăm: mười lăm, hai mươi lăm.

105 uses lẻ for the empty tens: một trăm lẻ năm.

1,000 is ngàn in the South (Hanoi: nghìn). Prices drop the zeros: 35.000đ = ba mươi lăm ngàn.

Half past is rưỡi: hai giờ rưỡi. Phone numbers are read digit by digit, 0 = không.
